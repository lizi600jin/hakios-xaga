## Limits and retained history

This proves a bounded root-only character snapshot and three kernel vn_rdwr calls for the
same physical4096-byte LBA1. It does not prove a userland consumer, persistent whole-disk
block service, filesystem mount, throughput, link gear/lane, or globally exclusive ownership.
CRC32 agreement is32-bit checksum agreement, not SHA256 or mathematical byte equality.
The raw sense contents remain uninterpreted.718 remains PASS;716/717 remain NOT_PROVEN,
and716's exact rejected response field remains unknown. CPU713 and GPU issues are not fixed.

Quiesce publishes through cached atomics/aligned sharedDRAM only. The audit is generated by
loader after its accepted double-read gate snapshot and appended as the second span to the
existing HLOG. The entire body is therefore not exclusively kernel-generated. One save call
can make multiple WRITE10 commands; no claim of only one physical storage write is made.
Production source was not merged/pushed. Old BUILD/images/source and GPUEB WIP are preserved.
